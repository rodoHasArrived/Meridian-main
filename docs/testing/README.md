## Synthetic scenario contract

Default tests and demonstrations use seeded, deterministic synthetic scenarios. A scenario is acceptable when it fixes, in checked-in data or code:

- the institutions, parties, and accounts (synthetic identifiers only, no real account data),
- the market events, prices, corporate actions, statements, and broker events, with a fixed as-of date and seed,
- the expected outcomes with known values: holdings, cost basis, journals (balanced), reconciliation differences, and close balances.

Re-running a scenario must produce identical results. Existing examples:

- [`tests/fixtures/portfolio/mixed-credit-status-set.json`](../../tests/fixtures/portfolio/mixed-credit-status-set.json), generated by `generate_mixed_credit_status_set.py` next to it, with `source-payloads/` (synthetic funds, 50 direct loans, fixed income, equity, credit facility, as of 2026-06-04).
- Golden fixtures under `tests/fixtures/corporate-actions/golden`, `fund-economics/golden`, `schedules/golden`, and `security-master/` (terms, schedule validation).
- `tests/Meridian.Tests/Reconciliation/Fixtures` for reconciliation inputs.
- The seeded W10 population recorded in [`evidence/w10-615abde9/population.json`](evidence/w10-615abde9/population.json) (historical, commit-bound).

Simulated success proves Meridian's behavior against its own contract. It is not evidence of provider or custodian behavior; use the `synthetic-verified` and `live-untested` labels from the [solo-developer workflow](../ai/solo-developer-workflow.md#provider-evidence-labels).

Follow-ups (not done here): no single index lists every scenario with its expected holdings, basis, journals, reconciliation differences, and close balances; the mixed-credit set has no documented end-to-end expected-close-balance assertion linked from this folder; scenario seeds are not uniformly recorded.
