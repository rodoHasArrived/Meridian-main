OptionChainTable — trading-grade option chain in the classic straddle layout: call columns | strike ladder | put columns (mirrored, bid/ask adjacent to the strike). ITM cells carry the accent alpha-10 wash (calls below spot, puts above), a spot marker row shows where the underlying trades, and greeks/IV/OI columns are configurable. One tab stop — ↑/↓ move the focused strike, Enter/Space selects. Use it wherever a chain preview needs to be a real trading surface (Covered-Call Lab, options research) instead of a generic table.
