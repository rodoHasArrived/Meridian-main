CandleChart — candlestick price chart with MA overlays, optional volume subpane, and a crosshair price chip. Up/down = --chart-equity/--chart-drawdown. Renders through ChartFrame: real-pixel geometry, `height`/`rail` props, `priceFmt` (default Fmt.price). Live crosshair via ChartCursor.useChartCrosshair(count, {padLeft:10, rail:64}).
