Treemap — squarified area map of positive weights (exposure by position/sector, allocation by book). Tile area ∝ `value`; tiles are alpha washes with hairline borders. Colors by day-change `delta` (green/red, intensity ∝ magnitude), by `group` (fixed wash palette), or flat. Labels hide progressively as tiles shrink; full detail in the title tooltip. Optional `onSelect` makes tiles clickable.
