WaterfallChart — additive bridge from a starting level through signed contributions to a net (P&L attribution: gross → carry → funding → fees → net; NAV bridges). Items are `start` (absolute), `delta` (floats from the running level — green up, red down), or `total` (drops to zero, accent); a computed Net bar is appended unless one is provided. Dashed connectors carry the level across gaps. Flat, token-driven.
