SelectionCompare — comparison by selection. Put `CompareToggle({item:{id,title,tag,values}})` on a grid row, figure series or ledger line; `CompareTray` (inline or `docked="corner"`) shows the growing set with chips, Clear and `Compare N` (from two); `CompareSheet` renders the set as a PlateGrid under the comparison-plate contract (one shared domain and tick set, plates never resized). Wrap in `SelectionCompareProvider({max, categories, domain, format})`.
