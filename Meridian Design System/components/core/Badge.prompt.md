Compact tag badge mirroring the desktop `NeutralBadgeStyle` + semantic badge styles — small-caps, 4px radius, alpha-10 fill with a solid semantic border and dim text. Not a soft pill.

```jsx
<Badge variant="live" dot>Live</Badge>
<Badge variant="paper">Paper</Badge>
<Badge variant="success" dot>Healthy</Badge>
<Badge variant="warning">Stale</Badge>
```

Variants: `neutral | info | success | warning | danger` and the environment modes `live | paper | fixture`. Labels are short — they render as small-caps.
