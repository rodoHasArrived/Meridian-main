Raw --green is the one hue that lands marginally under 4.5:1 as body text — which is exactly why semantic labels render in the darker -dim variant. Focus ring (accent on panel) is 5.5:1, well over the 3:1 non-text minimum.

The rule: semantic text uses the -dim variant; raw hues are for borders & ≥18px icons. Follow the colors-states trio and you are conformant by construction.

Keyboard

Tab follows DOM order — no positive tabindex anywhere.

Ctrl/⌘ K opens the command palette from anywhere.

Esc closes every overlay — Dialog, Drawer, Modal, palette — and focus returns to the trigger.

Arrows rove within Select, Tree, Tabs, Radio, Menu; container is one tab stop.

Semantics & motion

Overlays are role="dialog" + aria-modal, labelled by title. Toasts are aria-live polite. Breadcrumb carries aria-current.

Icon-only buttons require an aria-label; decorative glyphs are aria-hidden.

Every continuous motion honors prefers-reduced-motion.
