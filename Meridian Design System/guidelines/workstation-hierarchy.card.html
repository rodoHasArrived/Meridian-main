Layout is free — columns, rails, or bottom sheets. Order is not: context above object, evidence before consequence, consequence before action, at 390 px and at 1920 px alike.

Rules that make it hold
  • Context is declared once and inherited. No panel below the bar carries its own period, currency, or basis control.
  • The object is named once. One ObjectHeader per screen; a second identity block below it is a defect.
  • Evidence is persistent, not modal. An evidence rail that only opens as a dialog forces a choice between the claim and its support.
  • Consequence precedes action. An enabled primary button with no stated consequence is the system’s most expensive defect.
  • One primary action per plane. Two equal primary actions means two screens.
  • Every layer degrades honestly. Evidence retains its last valid snapshot under ContinuityFrame; blocked actions name the unmet guard.
