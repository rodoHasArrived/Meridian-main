01  OVERVIEW {{ asOf }}

The accounting domain turns positions and postings into statements a reviewer can sign. Eighteen components share one money primitive, so every figure in the system rounds, aligns, and reads the same way — from a single amount in a cell to a period-end trial balance that proves itself.

WHEN TO USE
  • The shape is a statement, not a list of rows.
  • A figure must tie to another figure and the arithmetic has to be visible.
  • Negatives, currencies, and rounding carry consequences.
  • The surface will be printed, exported, or signed off.
WHAT IT REPLACES
  • Generic data tables carrying money in body text.
  • Per-screen currency formatting and ad-hoc rounding.
  • Totals computed in the view with no proof of balance.
  • Red and green applied by hand, inconsistently, per team.
