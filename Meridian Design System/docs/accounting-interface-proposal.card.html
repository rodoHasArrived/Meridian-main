Reorganizing the books — scope and obligation over artifact

4 books templates · 26 accounting modules
full report: docs/ACCOUNTING_INTERFACE_4_2.md

The accounting interface is organized by artifact — a ledger view, a TB view, a recon screen — while the work is organized by scope (period × basis × entity) and obligation (what must tie, post, close). Period is a muted text line; basis has no control; the close is a place, not a state; tie-out, posting impact, and materiality exist as screens you visit, not states you carry. Three reorganizations, six concepts.
