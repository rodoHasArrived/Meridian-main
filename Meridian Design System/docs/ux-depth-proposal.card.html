Proposal · not law · baseline 3.5.0

Deepening the layer around the decision

268 exports · 26 templates
167 adopted · 101 with no template

The decision layer is finished work — guarded flows, reversibility classes, evidence and consequence rails, twelve time roles, ten data conditions. What is thin is the layer around the decision: how an operator arrives, resumes, keeps attention, submits, and returns. Every item below is stated as a gap with evidence, drawn from adoption-census-4.0.json, CONTRACT_AUDIT.md §D, and BROWSER_TEST_PLAN.md. Full report: docs/UX_DEPTH_4_1.md.
